In quarters without a conversion, Annualized and Full Coverage payments are lower than Safe Harbor. Conversion quarters catch up. Green = less than safe harbor · Red = more than safe harbor.
How to read this
Safe Harbor — 4 equal payments totaling 110% of your 2025 tax. No penalty risk. May over- or under-pay vs. actual.
Annualized Min — minimum payments under IRS Schedule AI to avoid underpayment penalty. Low in non-conversion quarters. Q4 (Jan 15) includes the full remaining balance — nothing left at filing.
Full Coverage — same timing but sized to pay your entire year's tax. Q4 covers everything — nothing due at filing.
Federal safe harbor = lesser of 110% of 2025 tax OR 90% of 2026 tax. NJ = lesser of 100% of 2025 tax OR 80% of 2026 NJ tax. Annualized = IRS Schedule AI / NJ-2210 principles (22.5% factor). These are estimates — consult your CPA before making payments.
